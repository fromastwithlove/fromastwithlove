Privacy policy

Mini · effective 15 November 2026

Who uses Mini

Mini is a private assistant with a single user, its owner. It isn't offered to anyone else.

What Mini accesses

With the owner's consent, through Google's Gmail API, Mini reads the owner's email, manages labels on it and creates drafts. It doesn't send email and doesn't delete it.

How the data is used

Only to help the owner: to summarise mail, sort it with labels, point out what needs an answer, and prepare drafts. Mini's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is processed and kept

On the owner's own computer, by a language model that runs on that computer. Summaries and drafts are shown to the owner in the owner's private chat in a messenger (Telegram). Nothing is shared with anyone else, sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Mini's access at any time in their Google Account, under Third-party connections.
